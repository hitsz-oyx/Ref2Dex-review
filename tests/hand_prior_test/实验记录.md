# Hand-pose prior experiment record（2026-07-15/16）

## Question

We tested whether whole-hand pose can improve object-hand correspondence beyond
the existing local geometry. The production reference was B: a joint 11-D PTv3
with object-conditioned dense hand-contact BCE at weight `0.005`.

## Evidence

1. On smoke12, directly broadcasting a 32-D pooled `g_hand` before PTv3 made
   the random-edge QFL worse than B, despite slightly improving the auxiliary
   heatmap. A late-gated identity-start version did not improve QFL either.
2. The H-only probe used only canonical-to-runtime hand-surface displacement;
   it held out complete sequences and controlled object identity and hand side.
   Across 44 groups / 4,332 held-out frames, object-conditioned mean MSE was
   `0.073496` versus pose-kNN `0.088255` for topology, and `0.073537` versus
   `0.086974` for anchors. There is no generalizable `P(contact | H)` prior.
3. The zero-initialized edge residual tested
   `logit_ij = logit_local_ij + Delta(edge_ij, a_j, g_hand)`. On smoke12,
   batch-rolled `g_hand` was best on QFL/P99, proving that those gains were
   conservative zero calibration rather than correct whole-hand information.
4. The clean full-data warm-start pair used the same B checkpoint, froze B for
   1,000 steps, and then jointly fine-tuned to 5,000 steps. At step 3,000 the
   matched vector was slightly ahead on QFL/nonzero error, but at step 5,000
   the `g_hand=0` control was better in all five metrics:

| Step | Variant | QFL ↓ | zero P99 ↓ | nonzero MAE ↓ | strong MAE ↓ | perturb QFL ↓ |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3,000 | `g_hand=0` | 0.0016158 | **0.1779334** | 0.1672827 | 0.6364910 | 0.0018226 |
| 3,000 | matched | **0.0015762** | 0.1800275 | **0.1667271** | **0.6324428** | **0.0017850** |
| 5,000 | `g_hand=0` | **0.0017764** | **0.2210954** | **0.1543671** | **0.5831983** | **0.0019577** |
| 5,000 | matched | 0.0018702 | 0.2244404 | 0.1545299 | 0.5836295 | 0.0020514 |

## Decision

Do not add pooled `g_hand` through early concatenation, late token gating, or
direct additive edge residuals. The retained baseline is B. A future study, if
needed, should only test a candidate-gated residual and report strong/nonzero
error under a fixed zero-P99 budget.
